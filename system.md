# Welcome to EduVoice: Empowering Education Through Technology

Welcome to **EduVoice**! This guide is designed to help you understand what this platform is all about and how you can use it to enhance your learning, collaborate with peers, and report issues transparently. 

Built for the **GMC Internal Hackathon 2026**, EduVoice specifically tackles the psychological barriers that prevent shy students from participating in class and reporting grievances.

---

## What is EduVoice?

EduVoice is a collaborative educational platform. It brings students, teachers, and institution administrators together in one digital space. It goes beyond just sharing class notes; it provides a vibrant community where you can ask questions without fear of judgment, report issues with transparency, and track your academic progress.

---

## 🎭 User Roles: Who Are You?

When you log into the platform, your experience is tailored to your specific role:

1. **Student** 🎓
   - *Your Goal:* Learn, collaborate, and grow.
   - *What you can do:* View your enrolled subjects, submit assignments, ask questions (anonymously or publicly) in the Q&A forum, and report grievances to administration with tracking codes.

2. **Teacher** 👨‍🏫
   - *Your Goal:* Guide and evaluate students.
   - *What you can do:* Create assignments, grade student submissions, answer student questions in the Q&A feed, and view engagement analytics on how anonymous features are empowering your shy students.

3. **Institution Admin** 🏢
   - *Your Goal:* Manage your school or college's operations.
   - *What you can do:* Create semesters, add subjects, assign teachers, and review/resolve student grievances specific to your institution.

4. **Super Admin** 👑
   - *Your Goal:* Oversee the entire platform.
   - *What you can do:* Manage all institutions, view platform-wide statistics, and manage overall system settings.

---

## 🌟 Core Features: What Can You Do?

Here is a breakdown of the main tools available to you on the platform:

### 1. 💬 Trackable Anonymous Q&A
Stuck on a problem but afraid to ask? The Q&A forum is the place to go!
- **Ask Questions:** Ask questions specific to a subject or general questions for everyone.
- **Barrier-Free Anonymity:** Feeling shy? You can choose to post questions or answers *anonymously*. The system will generate a fun, random, persistent name for you (like *CuriousPanda42*).
- **Tracking Tokens:** If you ask a question anonymously, you receive a unique tracking token (e.g., `QA-X8H2B1`). Use this token to check for answers anytime without logging in or revealing who you are!

### 2. 🚨 Grievance Management
A transparent way to report issues to your institution.
- **Submit Grievances:** Report academic, facility, or administrative issues. You can even attach photos or videos as evidence.
- **Track Status:** Just like the Q&A, you can get a tracking token to see exactly who is handling your grievance and when it gets resolved.
- **Modals:** Everything happens smoothly on one page without annoying page reloads.

### 3. 📚 Assignments & Submissions
A streamlined way to handle coursework.
- **For Teachers:** Create assignments, set due dates, review student work, and assign grades (scores).
- **For Students:** Easily see what assignments are due, upload your files or text, and view your grades once the teacher has reviewed them.

### 4. 📊 Engagement Dashboards
When you log in, you are greeted with a customized, premium dashboard:
- **Students** see their enrolled subjects, recent assignments, and upcoming deadlines.
- **Teachers** see statistics on how many questions are being asked, how many assignments need grading, and a beautiful Chart.js visualization of student engagement.
- **Admins** see high-level statistics like the number of active grievances and users.

## 🛡️ Privacy and Safety

- **Anonymous Mode:** We understand that asking questions or reporting issues can sometimes be intimidating. Our anonymous mode protects your real identity while still giving you a voice in the community.
- **Data Separation:** If there are multiple schools (institutions) using the platform, data is strictly separated. Students and teachers only see information relevant to their own institution.

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Log In:** Use your email and password to access the platform.
2. **Check Your Dashboard:** Your dashboard is your home base. It will show you what needs your attention today.
3. **Explore:** Click around! Visit the Q&A forum to see what others are discussing, or check out your pending assignments.
